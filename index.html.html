What is Swagbucks?

Swagbucks is a rewards platform where eligible members may earn points (often called SB) through activities offered on the platform. Depending on availability, activities can include surveys, shopping, games, and other offers. Points and reward options are subject to the platform’s current terms.

Rewards are not guaranteed income. The activities available to you can vary by country, account, device, and offer.

Who can participate?

Eligibility depends on the service and the specific offer. Before proceeding, check the current requirements shown by Swagbucks and the offer provider.

  • Confirm the offer is available in your country.
  • Check the minimum age and account requirements.
  • Make sure your iPhone and iOS version meet the offer terms.
  • Check whether the offer is restricted to new users.

Important

Availability, rewards, and qualifying actions can vary. Do not assume an app install alone qualifies unless the specific offer terms say so.

Frequently asked questions

Does every app install earn a reward?

No. Only actions that meet the specific offer’s requirements may qualify. Tracking, eligibility, and other conditions can affect approval.

Is Swagbucks available to everyone?

No. Availability and individual offer eligibility vary by location and offer terms. Check the official source before participating.
